## Superscripts and Subscripts

**Superscripts:**
- $^c$ = compression
- $^t$ = tension
- $^f$ = flexure
- $^s$ = shear

**Subscripts:**
- $L$ = longitudinal direction
- $T$ = transverse direction
- $w$ = web
- $f$ = flange
- $x$ = x-component (strong axis)
- $y$ = y-component (weak axis)
- $b$ = bolt
- $br$ = bearing
- $nt$ = net tension
- $cr$ = critical buckling

## Notes

1. **Longitudinal (L) direction**: Direction of pultrusion (primary fiber orientation)
2. **Transverse (T) direction**: Perpendicular to pultrusion direction
3. **Characteristic value**: Statistically determined property (75% confidence, 20% exclusion limit) per ASTM D6121 or D7290
4. **Adjusted values**: Material properties modified by environmental factors ($C_M$, $C_T$, $C_{CH}$) and time effect factor ($\lambda$)
5. **Units**: Primary units are ksi and inches; SI units in parentheses (MPa and mm)
