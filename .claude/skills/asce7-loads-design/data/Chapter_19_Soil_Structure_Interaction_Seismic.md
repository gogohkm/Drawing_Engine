**19.1.1 Scope** Foundation of the design earthquake forces (SSI) is a phenomenon that influences the response of buildings and their foundation. This chapter establishes procedure and permitted to calculate the effects of soil-structure interaction (SSI) on the design response of buildings. The considerations of SSI as defined by the provisions of this chapter are permitted to modify the design forces and calculated foundations in accordance with the provisions of this chapter. Design forces and SSI permitted foundations is not typically, for the purposes of this chapter, provided that consideration of soil-structure interaction is considered. The zone that results in the smaller reduction of seismic design demands shall be evaluated. Where forces determined using considerations of SSI are to apply in lieu of forces determined without considerations of SSI, the structure is located on Site Class C, D, E, or F, SSI base shear and seismic response coefficient shall be employed to augment the design forces per Section 12.8.

If the provisions of this chapter are used, then Section 12.8.1.3 shall not be employed to establish $T_a$ for the calculation of the seismic response coefficient, $C_s$, or for other purposes.

**19.1.2 Symbols** The following symbols apply only to the provisions of Chapter 19 as indicated and are in addition to the definitions of Chapter 11:

**FOUNDATION INPUT MOTION:** Motion that effectively drives the foundation of a structure without regard to the effects of the foundation system.

**KINEMATIC INTERACTION:** The interaction that can be significant for deeply embedded foundation and more. Foundation input motion can be significantly different from the free-field motion.

**KINEMATIC SSI:** The modification of base-field ground motion and the response of the foundation relative to the site conditions.

**RADIATION DAMPING:** The damping is the soil-structure system that accounts for the propagation and dissipation of waves away from the foundation. Radiation damping augments hysteretic damping of the foundation relative to the free-field motion and the damping in the structure.

## 19.2.2 Linear Dynamic Analysis

The inclusion of kinematic interaction and inertial interaction analysis in accordance with Section 19.2.1 is permitted. The kinematic interaction is permitted to be accounted for by using the free-field response spectrum, per Section 19.3.1. The inertial interaction is permitted to be represented by soil spring and dashpot properties per Section 19.3.2. If either site-specific ground motion analysis or GMPE is used, the period shall be modified as described in Section 19.3.2, and the equivalent-elastic force-deformation characteristics permitted by Section 19.3.2 shall be used instead of the effective viscous damping ratio of the soil-structure system.

The model base shear calculated with the effects of SSI, $V_{SSI}$, shall be determined as

$$V_{SSI} = \max(0.7V, V')$$ (19.2-4)

where

$$V' = \text{Modified base shear} = V_f \left(\frac{T}{T_f}\right)^2$$ for $T > T_f$, and

$$V' = V_f \text{ for } T \leq T_f$$, and

$$S_a = S_{aT}/(R_{μ}B_T)$$ for $T > T_f$, and

$$S_a = S_{aT}/B_T$$ for $T \leq T_f$$, and

where $T_s$ and $B_T$ are defined in Section 11.4.7, $R_{μ}$ = 1, and $T$ = fundamental period of the structure, and $T_f$ is the period of the structure as defined in Section 19.2.1. The inertial interaction modification of Section 19.3.2 is not necessary to be used with the kinematic interaction modifications of Section 19.2.2 when the site-specific response spectrum $S_a$ is applied directly to the structure without consideration of the base-fixity effects of the soil-structure system.

### 19.2.2.2 SSI Site-Specific Response Spectrum

A site-specific response spectrum that incorporates modifications caused by SSI is permitted to be developed in accordance with Section 21.2. These modifications can be applied in the following conditions:

1. Where the response spectrum is developed through a site-specific ground motion analysis in accordance with Section 21.1 or obtained using a ground motion prediction equation (GMPE) per Section 21.2,

2. For structures embedded in the ground, the site-specific response spectrum should be developed at the depth of embedment or both base slab averaging or embedment, or both base slab averaging and embedment effect are explicitly modeled, or both base slab averaging and embedment effects are explicitly modeled in accordance with Section 19.3.

3. For structures embedded in the ground, the site-specific response spectrum shall be developed at the depth of embedment unless both base slab averaging and embedment effects are explicitly modeled in accordance with Section 19.3.1.
