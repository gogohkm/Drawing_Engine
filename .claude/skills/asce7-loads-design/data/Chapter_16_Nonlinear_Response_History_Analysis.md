**16.1.1 Scope** This chapter establishes minimum criteria and requirements for the structural design of buildings when nonlinear response history analysis (NRHA) is used to determine acceptability criteria, stiffness, and ductility of the seismic force-resisting system (SFRS). Nonlinear response history analysis is permitted for Seismic Design Categories B through F. Nonlinear response history analysis, to demonstrate acceptable strength, stiffness, and ductility, the requirements of Section 16.1.2. Nonlinear response history analysis is not permitted for the design of structures assigned to Seismic Design Category A.

Demonstration of the design and analysis shall be prepared in accordance with the structural requirements of Chapters 11 and 12 and the minimum loading requirements of this standard. Where NRHA is permitted by the applicable material design standard or where specifically permitted by the applicable material design standard and analyzed in accordance with the criteria in Section 16.1. Analysis procedures shall comply with the requirements and limitations of Section 16. For designs of structures assigned to Seismic Design Categories C through F, the analysis shall meet all applicable criteria of Chapter 11 and 12.

**16.1.2 Linear Analysis** In addition to nonlinear response history analysis, the following linear analysis shall be performed in accordance with the requirements of Chapter 12:

1. Sections 12.8, 12.9, 12.10.3, 12.12.5, and 12.12.6 as well as 12.13.3 as not apply to the linear analysis. Where seismic response modification factors or response values are assigned to Seismic Design Category A.
2. Acceleration floor response spectra in accordance with Section 13.3 or 16.3.3. For structures over 160 ft (50 m) in structural height:
3. Seismic loads on elements required to be modeled in the structure shall be derived using the analysis.

**16.1.3 Vertical Response Analysis** Nonlinear response history analysis is permitted to be used in conjunction with a horizontal equivalent static force procedure or a modal response spectrum analysis to determine the vertical seismic effects, provided that all of the following criteria are met:

1. Vertical elements of the gravity force-resisting system are included in the NRHA model.
2. Linear static analysis is performed to establish gravity load effects on the structure.
3. Consideration of vertical earthquake effects.

**16.1.4 Documentation** Before performing the analytical evaluation, a comprehensive plan for the independent structural design review shall be prepared to establish the independent structural design review methodology and the Authority

Having Jurisdiction. The project-specific criteria shall identify the following:

1. Independent verification of the structural design systems and procedures used in the structural design.
2. Procedures used to determine stiffness and characteristics.
3. Material property limits to be used, and sources and methods of substantive validation.
4. Testing of structural elements.
5. Description of the sequence of analysis building model
6. Sequence of laboratory test and other applicable data used to calibrate the nonlinear model, including all trial to failure testing.
7. Method to demonstrate reasonable and acceptable performance of elements of the seismic force-resisting system. Ground motions used in the analysis shall be established per Section 16.2.
8. Acceptance criteria used to determine acceptable system performance.
9. Where static control 1.05% of the values permitted is required to be recorded to determine acceptable performance.
10. A detailed summary of the analysis results and the independent structural design review used to determine acceptable performance.

Evidence, completion of the analysis review, the following documentation shall be established during the independent structural design review and communicated to the Authority Having Jurisdiction in accordance with Section 16.1.4:

1. Final project-level report, including and above acceptance criteria, analysis summary report(s), recommended future investigations, including both the target spectra and selection methodology used to analyze the system.
2. Documentation demonstrating that the building natural frequencies, building stiffness, properties, and structural capacity are properly represented.
3. Key structural system response parameters (results and documentation shall be established for communication with the Authority Having Jurisdiction in accordance with Section 16.4.
