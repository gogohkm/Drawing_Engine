## 31.2 TEST CONDITIONS

Wind tunnel tests, or similar tests using other fluids, used in whole or in part as the basis for the design wind loads for any building or other structure, shall be in accordance with this section. Tests for the determination of mean and dynamic wind loads shall be representative of the structure under consideration in accordance with Sections 31.2.1 through 31.2.4.

**31.2.1 Freestanding Solid Freestanding Signs.** Wind tunnel test data for freestanding solid freestanding signs shall be representative of appropriate upwind terrain surface roughness in accordance with Section 26.7.3.

**31.2.2 Buildings.** Wind tunnel test data shall be representative of terrain surface roughness as specified in Section 31.4.4.

## 31.3 DYNAMIC RESPONSE

Tests to determine forces and/or pressures shall include the dynamic response of the structure in a boundary layer flow that simulates the natural atmospheric boundary layer described in Section 26.7.3.

## 31.5 LOAD EFFECTS FOR BUILDING, OTHER STRUCTURES, AND MINIMUM DESIGN LIVE LOADS

**31.5.1 Wind Loads.** Wind loads on buildings, other structures, and components at a specific site shall be permitted to be determined using a recognized wind tunnel test procedure in accordance with Section 31.2 or an equivalent approach that permits the site and its immediate surroundings, wind tunnel data analysis, wind tunnel test procedures, and topography tests (wind directionality factor, wind speed-up effects, and wind-tunnel testing on and proximity to buildings) and combination to be determined.

**31.5.1.1 Limitations on Wind Loads for Buildings and Other Structures.** For Risk Category II buildings and other structures, minimum required loads shall be determined in accordance with Section 31.1.3 or Section 26.7.2.1 where applicable. For buildings and other structures in Risk Category III and IV, load test data determined in accordance with Section 31.4.1 and Section 31.4.2 shall be considered for exposures where the values represent the wind test procedure defined by Section 31.2 and shall also be clearly noted in the test report.

**31.5.1.2 Limitations on Wind Loads for Roofing Solar Collectors and Roof-Mounted Photovoltaic Panels.** Wind tunnel test data for roofing solar collectors and roof-mounted photovoltaic panels representing boundary layer model geometry and wind speed-up for all test geometry shall be subject to the directional effects of the resultant boundary layer model and wind speed-up for the modeled area or their equivalents.

The test procedures shall be based on the largest wind speed that results in the greatest wind loads for any wind direction at the site.

**31.5.1.2 Design for Tornado Loads Not Required.** For Risk Category III buildings and other structures in accordance with Section 31.1, minimum base wind test loads determined in accordance with Section 31.3.1 shall not be required where the exposure category 1 is defined in Section 31.4.2 where applicable. The minimum design wind loads for buildings or other structures in accordance with Section 31.4.2 shall not be less than the values of base wind loads determined in accordance with Sections 31.1.1 through 31.3.1.2.

**31.5.2 Limitations.** Where the embedded wind offset factors C_p(z), and (GC_p) are determined separately.
