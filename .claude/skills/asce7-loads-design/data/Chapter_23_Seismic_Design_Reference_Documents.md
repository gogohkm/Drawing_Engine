**23.1 CONSENSUS STANDARDS AND OTHER REFERENCE DOCUMENTS**

This section lists the consensus standards and other documents referenced in Chapter 11 that shall be considered part of the standard to the extent referenced throughout Chapter 11. The standards and other referenced documents listed in this chapter represent acceptable procedures for design and construction to resist the effects of earthquakes as described in Chapter 11.

**Alternate Means**: Where determination of compliance with the Seismic Peak Ground Glider Failure Point Displacement (Chapter 11), and the geotechnical report identifies conditions that the engineer-of-record can review, assess, and consider in the design, other documents may be used subject to the approval of the authority having jurisdiction (AHJ).
