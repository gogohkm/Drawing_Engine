**ASCE 7-22 Minimum Design Loads and Associated Criteria for Buildings and Other Structures**

**29.1.1 Structure Type.** This chapter applies to the determination of design wind loads on the MWFRS for the following types of structures and other structures:

- Rooftop structures and equipment for buildings of all heights,
- Solid freestanding walls and solid signs, chimneys, tanks, circular bins, silos, open signs, trussed towers and similar structures that do not meet the requirements of Section 26.2,
- Other structures not covered in Chapters 27 and 28.

Use Notes: Use Chapter 29 to determine wind pressures on the MWFRS for rooftop equipment, rooftop solar panels, ground-mounted freestanding solid panel systems, and ground-mounted flat-topped solar panels at a mean roof height less than or equal to 60 ft (18.3 m).

**User Note:** Procedures in Chapter 30 are available for C&C of ground-mounted freestanding solar panel systems and ground-mounted flat-topped solar panel systems.

**29.1.2 Conditions.** An appurtenance or structure that aligns with the conditions listed below is within the scope of this chapter. The chapter applies to:

1. Structure is a regular-shaped structure as defined in Section 26.2 and

2. Structure does not have response characteristics making it subject to across-wind loading, vortex shedding, instability caused by galloping or flutter, or does not have a site condition that falls under one of the categories of Section 26.5.1(b), does not have a site subject to channeling effects or buffeting in the wake of upwind obstructions.

**29.1.3 Limitations.** The provisions of this chapter take into account the load magnification effect caused by gusts in resonance with along-wind vibrations of flexible buildings and structures that do not exceed the requirements of Section 26.2 for height, height-to-width ratios, and natural frequency, or where a more detailed analysis is made. For structures not meeting these requirements, refer to recognized literature for determination of design loads. The load magnification effect of torque and other structures in certain features.

**29.1.4 Shielding.** There shall be no reductions in velocity pressures caused by apparent shielding afforded by buildings and other structures or terrain features.

## 29.2 GENERAL REQUIREMENTS

The general requirements established in Section 28, including Sections 26.1 through 26.3, shall apply in determining wind loads for buildings and other structures and their components in accordance with Chapter 29.

The following wind load parameters shall be determined in accordance with the applicable:
- Basic wind speed, $V$, Section 26.5;
- Wind directionality factor, $K_d$, Section 26.6;
- Exposure Category: $B$, $C$, or $D$, see Sections 26.7;
- Topographic effects, $K_{zt}$, Section 26.8; and
- Gust-effect factor, $G$ or $G_f$, Section 26.11;
- Enclosure classification (Section 26.12).

## 29.3 DESIGN WIND LOADS: SOLID FREESTANDING WALLS AND SOLID FREESTANDING SIGNS

**29.3.1 Solid Freestanding Walls and Solid Freestanding Signs.** The design wind pressure for the MWFRS for solid freestanding walls and solid freestanding signs shall be determined by the following formula:

$$p = q_h G C_f \tag{29.3-1}$$

where

$$F = q_h G C_f A_s(h) \tag{29.3-1.SI}$$

where

$q_h =$ Velocity pressure evaluated at height $z$ as defined in Section 26.10 in accordance with Equation 26.10-1.

$G =$ Gust-effect factor from Section 26.11.

$C_f =$ Net force coefficient from Figure 29.3-1 through 29.4-4 and Tables 29.5-1 and 29.5-2 where $G$ is taken as 0.85; for all other cases, the provisions of Section 26.11 shall apply.

$A_s =$ Gross area of the solid wall or sign, ft² (m²).

## 29.3.2 Solid Attached Signs

The design wind pressure on a solid sign attached to a building or structure, where the plan dimensions parallel and normal to the plane of the wall, and the sign base location, are small as compared with the overall dimensions of the wall shall be determined by the following equation:

$$p = q_h (GC_p) \tag{29.3-2}$$

where the symbols are as defined in Step 5 and Step 6.

The net pressure, $(GC_p)$, equal to 1.8, is to be applied normal to all surfaces of the sign.

Equation (29.3-2) accounts for a positive pressure on the windward face and negative face, $p$, and surface pressure coefficient (negative) on the leeward face. $K_d$ from Table 26.6-1 is appropriate for the category of the structure to which the sign is attached.

## 29.4 DESIGN WIND LOADS: OTHER STRUCTURES

**29.4.1 Rooftop Structures and Equipment for Buildings of All Heights; Calculation of Wind Loads.** The design wind force for rooftop structures and equipment on buildings shall be determined from the velocity pressure calculated at the top surface of the roof by Equation (29.4-1):

$$F = q_h G C_f A_f \tag{29.4-1}$$

where

$q_h =$ Velocity pressure evaluated at height $z$ as defined in Section 26.10 in accordance with Equation 26.10-1; and

$G =$ Gust-effect factor, see Section 26.11. See User Note in Section 29.3.1.

## 29.4 DESIGN WIND LOADS: OTHER STRUCTURES

**29.4.1 Rooftop Structures and Equipment for Buildings of All Heights; Calculation of Wind Loads.** The design wind force for rooftop structures and equipment on buildings shall be determined from the velocity pressure calculated at the top surface of the roof by Equation (29.4-1):

$$F = q_h G C_f A_f \text{ (N)} \tag{29.4-1.SI}$$

where

$q_h =$ Velocity pressure evaluated at height $z$ as defined in Section 26.10 in accordance with Equation 26.10-1;

$G =$ Gust-effect factor, see Section 26.11. See User Note in Section 29.3.1;

$A_f =$ Gross area of structure or appurtenance projected on a vertical plane normal to the wind direction;

$C_f =$ Force coefficient from Figures 29.4-1 through 29.4-4 and Tables 29.5-1 and 29.5-2 where $G$ is taken as 0.85; for all other structures, $G$ shall be determined from Section 26.11.

Determination of $G$, $C_f$, and $A_f$ for structures found in Figures 29.4-1 through 29.4-5 and Tables 29.5-1 and 29.5-2 shall be permitted to be reduced linearly from 1.0 for $L/h = 0.0$ to the value of $K_z$ at height $z$ is increased beyond the height of the structure, as shown with ASCE 7.

$(GC_p) = 1.8$ for rooftop structures and equipment with $h <$ less than $3.0$; $(h < 0.9 \text{ m})$, shall be permitted to be reduced linearly from 1.8 for $L/h = 0.0$ to the value of $K_z$ at height $z$ in increased beyond the height of the structure, as shown with ASCE 7.

The positive lateral force, $F_x$, and vertical force, $F_y$, for rooftop structures and equipment on structures shall be determined from Equation (29.4-1) or Equation (29.4-2) and Equation (29.4-3) for the combined effect on the roof surface and the parapet shall be determined from Equation (29.4-1) or Equation (29.4-1.SI).

The positive lateral force, $F_x$, shall be determined from the velocity pressure calculated at mean roof height by Equation (29.4-2):

$$F_x = q_h K_d (GC_{pn}) \text{ (lb)} \tag{29.4-2}$$

$$F_x = q_h K_d (GC_{pn}) \text{ (N)} \tag{29.4-2.SI}$$

where

$(GC_{pn}) = 1.8$ for rooftop structures and equipment with $h$ less than $3.0$ ft; $(h < 0.9 \text{ m})$ shall be permitted to be reduced linearly from 1.8 for $L/h = 0.0$ to the value of $K_z$ at height $z$ is increased beyond the height of the structure.

**29.4.2 External Walls of Rooftop Structures and Equipment on Buildings with Flat Roofs.** For external walls of rooftop structures and equipment with clearance height, $C$, above the roof or equal to the solid parapet height, $H$, above the roof or equal to $L/D$ considered at clearance height ground of less than or equal to 0.25 times the least horizontal dimension, $D$, of rooftop equipment or equipment is situated on a building with a flat roof slope $\leq 7°$, the total force on rooftop equipment or unit area $A_f$ shall include:

• Load from Figure 29.4-5 provided that all of the following conditions are met:

$L_{ry} \leq 6.7$ ft (2.04 m),

$\omega \leq 35°$,

$h_1 \leq 2$ ft (0.61 m),

$h_2 \leq 4$ ft (1.2 m), and

Equations (29.4-5) and Equation (29.4-5.SI) through Figure 29.4-7, the design wind pressure for rooftop solar panels apply to those located on enclosed or partially enclosed buildings of all heights with flat roofs, or with gable or hip roof slopes with $\theta \leq 7°$, with panels conforming to

$L_{ry} \leq 6.7$ ft (2.04 m),

$\omega \leq 35°$,

$h_1 \leq 2$ ft (0.61 m),

$h_2 \leq 4$ ft (1.2 m),

and with a minimum gap of 0.25 in. (6.4 mm) provided between all panels, and the spacing of gaps between panels not exceeding $6.7$ ft (2.04 m). In addition, the minimum horizontal clear distance between the panels and the edge of the roof shall be the larger of $2h_2$ and $4$ ft (1.2 m) for the design pressures in this section to apply. The design wind pressure for rooftop solar panels shall be determined by Equations (29.4-5) and (29.4-6):

$$p = q_h K_d (GC_{pn}) \text{ (lb/ft}^2\text{)} \tag{29.4-5}$$

$$p = q_h K_d (GC_{pn}) \text{ (N/m}^2\text{)} \tag{29.4-5.SI}$$

where $q_h$ is the velocity pressure for all surfaces evaluated at mean roof height, $h$, and $K_d$ is the wind directionality factor, see Section 26.6.

### Notes

1. Force coefficients for solid walls or signs with openings less than 30% of gross area shall be permitted to be multiplied by the reduction factor $(1 - (1 - \varepsilon)^{1.5})$.

2. To allow for both normal and oblique wind directions, the following cases shall be considered:

   For $s/h < 1$:

   **Case A:** Resultant force acts normal to the face of the wall or sign through the geometric center.

   **Case B:** Resultant force acts normal to the face of the wall or sign at a distance from the geometric center toward the windward edge equal to 0.2 times the average width of the wall or sign.

   For double-faced signs with all sides enclosed and $R_{\min} \leq 0.8B$, it is permitted that wind acts toward either face of the sign from an angle of incidence, $e = (0.2 - 0.25R_{\max})B$.

   For double-faced signs with all sides enclosed and $R_{\min} \leq 0.75$, it is permitted to multiply tabulated $C_f$ values in Cases A and B by the reduction factor, $(1 - 0.133R_{\min})$.

   For $B/s \geq 2$, Case B need not be considered, while Case C must be considered;

   **Case C:** Resultant forces act normal to the face of the wall or sign through the geometric centers of each region.

   For $s/h = 1$:

   The same cases as above except that the vertical locations of the resultant forces occur at a distance above the geometric center equal to 0.05 times the average height of the wall or sign.

3. For Case C where $s/h > 0.8$, for coefficients shall be multiplied by the reduction factor $(1.8 - s/h)$. It is permitted to apply this reduction with those specified in Note 2.

4. Linear interpolation is permitted for values of $s/h$, $B/s$, and $L_c/s$ other than shown.

1. Dynamic coefficients at a damping ratio, $\beta$, other than 1% damping (0.01) shall be calculated by multiplying the coefficients in this figure by $\sqrt{(0.01/\beta)}$ where $\beta \leq 0.05$.

2. Linear interpolation for intermediate values of effective wind area, $A$ shall be permitted.
