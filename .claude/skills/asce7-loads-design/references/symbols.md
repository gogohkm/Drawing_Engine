## Unit Conversions

### Pressure
- 1 psf = 47.88 Pa
- 1 kPa = 20.89 psf
- 1 psi = 144 psf

### Force
- 1 kip = 1,000 lbs
- 1 kN = 0.2248 kips
- 1 lb = 4.448 N

### Length
- 1 ft = 0.3048 m
- 1 in = 25.4 mm
- 1 mile = 5,280 ft

### Acceleration
- 1 g = 32.2 ft/s² = 386 in/s²
- 1 g = 9.81 m/s²
