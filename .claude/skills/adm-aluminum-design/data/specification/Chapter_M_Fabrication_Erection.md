# Chapter M Fabrication and Erection

## M.1 LAYOUT

A temperature correction shall be applied where necessary to achieve the designed dimensions at normal or operating temperature.

## M.2 CUTTING

### M.2.1 General

Cutting shall be by shearing, sawing, nibbling, routing, arc-cutting, laser cutting, or plasma cutting. Edges produced by cutting shall not be flamed to remove edge cracks. Oxygen cutting is prohibited.

### M.2.2 Edge Quality

The surface roughness profile of edges, including holes, flash-controlled holes, deburred holes and slots shall be 500 micro-inches (μm) or less. Edges shall be free of nicks and cracks.

### M.2.3 Reentrant Corners

Reentrant corners shall be filleted.

## M.3 HEATING

Temperatures exceeding 535°F, 500°F, 500°F, 515±5, and 540 and welldments made with filler metal alloys 5183, 5356, 5556, 5087, 5183, in the as-welded condition or when in the solution heat-treated condition shall not exceed 350°F at any point in welding when 1) designed using Organic strength
2) temper-designated 3) for alloys in 7XXX alloys during joining 4) for alloys in the specified temper except for base metals is specified in Section A.4.1.1.

## M.4 Fabrication Methods

### M.4.1 Bolts

The diameter of holes for bolts shall not exceed the nominal bolt diameter by more than 1/16 in. (2 mm). The sub-punched hole is less than that of the finished hole shall be at least 1/8 in. (3 mm) less than the diameter of the finished hole.

### M.4.2 Hole Alignment

Unfitting holes shall be in a manner that distorts the metal. All clips and flanges tighter between connected parts shall be removed before assembly.

## M.5 BENDING

Bend radii shall be large enough to avoid cracking.

## M.6 FINISHES

### M.6.1 Where Protective Coating Is Required

Protective finishes as specified should be applied protecting coating where:

a) alloy 2014 is in the presence of moisture,
b) Aluminum stored indoors that is exposed to wet site, c) Aluminum will be exposed to galvanic corrosion conditions,
c) Aluminum is exposed to corrosive compounds.

### M.6.2 Surface Preparation

Surfaces to be painted shall be prepared immediately before painting by:

a) Aluminum abrasive that blasts aluminum,
b) abrasion and acid cleaning in accordance with "Coil Coating" or
c) cleaning and pretreatment in accordance with aluminum finishers's coating,
d) Chemical conversion coating, or
e) Heavy hot alkalinizing coating.

Cleaned surfaces shall be painted within 8 hours of surface preparation. Materials left outdoors shall be dried completely before coating.

### M.6.3 Abrasion Blasting

Abrasion blasting shall be used except hard and soft steel or aluminum-oxide shot should be used in accordance with all surfaces. Air used for blasting shall be clean, dry in accordance with the ANSI/AWS C5.6. Sand, steel grit, or galvanized steel abrasives are prohibited.

## M.7 CONTACT WITH DISSIMILAR MATERIALS

As an alternative to the requirements of Section M.7.1 aluminum contacts other metals; except that, contact which is properly protected and shall be protected from the metal-to-metal contact by a primer or paint or coating.

### M.7.1 Aluminum in Contact with Steel

Where 1) aluminum contacts other metals except iron, steel, stainless steel, or where iron, steel, stainless steel contacts, cadmium- or zinc-plated or coated steel parts, or from contact by a electrical or mechanical separation.

Where an aluminum extrusion having a specified minimum tensile strength greater than 176 ksi (30 MPa) for bond loading including coil coating may be placed in contact with steel surfaces requiring a corrosion-resistant coating of a minimum rating.

### M.7.2 Wood, Fiberboard, or Other Porous Materials

Aluminum surfaces to be placed in contact with wood, fiberboard, or similar porous materials shall always water shall be painted.

## M.7.3 Concrete or Masonry

Aluminum surfaces shall be painted if they are to be placed in contact with concrete or masonry unless the concrete or masonry remains dry during curing and no corrosive additives such as chlorides are used. Aluminum shall not be embedded in concrete with corrosive additives such as chlorides if the aluminum is electrically connected to steel.

## M.8 FABRICATION TOLERANCES

Dimensional tolerances shall comply with the *Code of Standard Practice for Fabricating and Erecting Structural Aluminum*.

# RIGHT COLUMN

## M.9 WELDING

Welding shall comply with the AWS D1.2 *Structural Welding Code – Aluminum*. Filler alloys shall be selected from Tables M.9.1 and M.9.2.

The contract documents shall specify if visual inspection is required to be performed by AWS certified welding inspectors. When inspection other than visual inspection is required, the contract documents shall state the method, extent, inspector qualifications, and acceptance criteria for such inspection.

## M.10 BOLT INSTALLATION

Unless the joint is a slip-critical connection, bolts need only be installed snug tight, the tightness that exists when all plies in a joint are in firm but not necessarily continuous contact. Slip-critical connections shall be tightened and inspected in accordance with the RCSC *Specification for Structural Joints Using ASTM A325 or A490 Bolts*.

# RIGHT COLUMN

## M.11 RIVET INSTALLATION

### M.11.1 Driven Head

The driven heads of rivets shall be flat or cone-point, with dimensions as follows:

a) Flat heads shall have a diameter at least 1.4 times the nominal diameter of the rivet and a height at least 0.4 times the nominal diameter of the rivet.

b) Cone-point heads shall have a diameter at least 1.4 times the nominal diameter of the rivet and a height to the apex of at least 0.65 times the nominal diameter of the rivet. The nominal included angle at the apex of the cone shall be 127°.

### M.11.2 Hole Filling

Rivets shall fill holes completely. Rivet heads shall be concentric with the rivet holes and shall be in continuous contact with the surface of the part joined.

### M.11.3 Defective Rivets

Defective rivets shall be removed by drilling. The drill bit diameter shall not exceed the diameter of the replacement rivet.

## M.12 ERECTION TOLERANCES

Erection tolerances shall comply with the *Code of Standard Practice for Fabricating and Erecting Structural Aluminum*.
