## GLOSSARY

**allowable stress:** nominal strength divided by the safety factor.

**allowable stress design (ASD):** a method of proportioning structural members such that elastically computed stresses produced in the members by nominal loads do not exceed allowable stresses based on appropriate theory, relevant test data, and engineering experience (also called design by allowable stress).

**aluminum alloy:** a composition in which aluminum is the largest proportion of alloying elements, and its properties are modified by one or more alloying additions.

**aluminum alloy casting:** a casting containing aluminum and one or more alloying elements deliberately added to achieve specific composition, property and strength values.

**ASD** *(Allowable Strength Design)*: the method of proportioning structural members and their connections in accordance with the applicable load combinations, resistance factors, reliability index, or safety factors.

**beam:** a structural member that has one primary function of resisting bending loads.

**bolted connection:** a connection made by tightening bolt or nuts.

**buckling-type structure:** a structure of the type addressed by the design code, intended for allowable strength design.

**built-up member:** a member made by fastening together two or more shapes such as channels or angles to form a single load-carrying member.

**chord:** a longitudinal component part of a truss. (See Figure GL.1b.)

**column:** a structural member that has the primary function of carrying bending load, or induced load by torsional or eccentric or other loading modes.

**combined document:** documents that define the requirements of the parties that will be subjected by the contract into specification of requirements as one and the same specification documents, which is applicable.

**effective length:** ratio between the effective length of otherwise identical column with pinned ends and similar load.

**effective length factor:** ratio between effective length at beam-column end condition and length of otherwise identical column with pinned end condition.
