## Example Variant Meanings

**A suffix (e.g., II-1A, III-1A):**
- Usually Effective Width Method (EWM)
- Or ASD (Allowable Strength Design)
- Or first approach

**B suffix (e.g., II-1B, III-1B):**
- Usually Direct Strength Method (DSM)
- Or LRFD (Load and Resistance Factor Design)
- Or second approach

**C suffix (e.g., II-1C, III-7C):**
- Alternative method or section type
- Or third approach
- Check title for specific meaning
