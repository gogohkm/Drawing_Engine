# ASTM Steel Grades Quick Reference

Common steel grades for cold-formed steel design per AISI S100-16.

**Source:** Specification Chapter A (Material Properties), Volume 1 Part I Section 1

---

## ASTM A1003 - Structural Grade Steel

**Standard:** ASTM A1003/A1003M Standard Specification for Steel Sheet, Carbon, Metallic- and Nonmetallic-Coated for Cold-Formed Framing Members

| Grade | Fy (ksi) | Fu (ksi) | Coating Options | Typical Use |
|-------|----------|----------|-----------------|-------------|
| SS Grade 33 Type H | 33 | 45 | Bare, G40, G60, G90, A40, A60, AZ50 | Light-duty structural, studs |
| SS Grade 37 Type H | 37 | 52 | Bare, G40, G60, G90, A40, A60, AZ50 | Structural framing |
| SS Grade 40 Type H | 40 | 55 | Bare, G40, G60, G90, A40, A60, AZ50 | Structural framing |
| SS Grade 50 Type H | 50 | 65 | Bare, G40, G60, G90, A40, A60, AZ50 | **Most common structural** |
| SS Grade 50 Type L | 50 | 60 | Bare, G40, G60, G90, A40, A60, AZ50 | Lower ductility |
| SS Grade 80 Type H | 80 | 82 | Bare, G40, G60, G90 | High-strength applications |

**Notes:**
- Type H: Higher ductility (10% elongation min)
- Type L: Lower ductility (6% elongation min)
- Most examples use SS Grade 50 Type H

## ASTM A653 - Hot-Dip Galvanized Steel

**Standard:** ASTM A653/A653M Standard Specification for Steel Sheet, Zinc-Coated (Galvanized) or Zinc-Iron Alloy-Coated (Galvannealed) by the Hot-Dip Process

| Grade | Fy (ksi) | Fu (ksi) | Coating Designation | Typical Use |
|-------|----------|----------|---------------------|-------------|
| Grade 33 | 33 | 45 | G40, G60, G90, G100, G115, G140, G165, G185, G210 | Roof/wall panels, light framing |
| Grade 37 | 37 | 52 | G40, G60, G90, G100, G115, G140, G165, G185, G210 | Structural framing |
| Grade 40 | 40 | 55 | G40, G60, G90, G100, G115, G140, G165, G185, G210 | Structural framing |
| Grade 50 (Class 1) | 50 | 65 | G40, G60, G90, G100, G115, G140, G165, G185, G210 | **Common for structural** |
| Grade 50 (Class 3) | 50 | 60 | G40, G60, G90, G100, G115, G140, G165, G185, G210 | Lower ductility |
| Grade 80 | 80 | 82 | G40, G60, G90 | High-strength |

**Coating Designations:**
- G40 = 0.40 oz/ft² total both sides (light coating)
- G60 = 0.60 oz/ft² total both sides
- G90 = 0.90 oz/ft² total both sides (common)
- Higher numbers = thicker zinc coating (better corrosion resistance)

**Notes:**
- Most common: A653 Grade 50, G60 or G90 coating
- Used for outdoor applications (corrosion protection)

## ASTM A792 - Aluminum-Zinc Coated Steel

**Standard:** ASTM A792/A792M Standard Specification for Steel Sheet, 55% Aluminum-Zinc Alloy-Coated by the Hot-Dip Process

| Grade | Fy (ksi) | Fu (ksi) | Coating Designation | Typical Use |
|-------|----------|----------|---------------------|-------------|
| Grade 33 | 33 | 45 | AZ50, AZ55 | Roof panels |
| Grade 37 | 37 | 52 | AZ50, AZ55 | Roof/wall panels |
| Grade 50 | 50 | 65 | AZ50, AZ55 | **Standing seam roofs** |
| Grade 80 | 80 | 82 | AZ50, AZ55 | High-strength roofing |

**Coating Composition:**
- 55% Aluminum
- 43.4% Zinc
- 1.6% Silicon

**Coating Designations:**
- AZ50 = 0.50 oz/ft² total both sides
- AZ55 = 0.55 oz/ft² total both sides

**Notes:**
- Better corrosion resistance than galvanized (especially coastal)
- Common for architectural metal roofing
- Also available with Zn-5% Al coating (not as common)

## Thickness Ranges

**Typical cold-formed steel thickness:**
- **Minimum:** 0.0179 in (28 gauge, reference thickness per AISI)
- **Maximum:** 0.3 in (practical limit for cold-forming)
- **Common range:** 14-20 gauge (0.075" - 0.036")

**Gauge equivalents:**
| Gauge | Thickness (in) | Design Thickness (in)* |
|-------|----------------|------------------------|
| 14 | 0.0747 | 0.0713 |
| 16 | 0.0598 | 0.0566 |
| 18 | 0.0478 | 0.0451 |
| 20 | 0.0359 | 0.0346 |
| 22 | 0.0299 | 0.0283 |
| 24 | 0.0239 | 0.0226 |

*Design thickness = base steel thickness - coating thickness (for G90)

## Ductility Requirements

**Per Specification Chapter A:**

**Structural members:**
- Elongation ≥ 10% in 2 inches (for members)
- Elongation ≥ 7% in 2 inches (minimum for connections)

**Special cases:**
- Grade 80: Elongation ≥ 10% required
- Cold-work may increase Fy but reduce ductility

---

## Cold-Work Strength Increase

**Per Specification Section A3.3.2:**

Corner regions of cold-formed sections may have increased yield strength due to cold-working.

**Full section average Fy:**

F_ya = (C F_yc B_c + F_y B_v) / (B_c + B_v)

where:
- F_yc = corner yield strength (increased)
- F_y = virgin (flat) yield strength
- B_c = total corner length
- B_v = total flat length
- C = factor (typically 1.0 to 1.2 for calculation)

**Note:** See Example I-16 for detailed calculation

## Common Design Scenarios

### Roof Purlins/Girts (Examples II-1A, II-1B, II-1C)
- **Grade:** ASTM A653 Grade 50 (Fy = 50 ksi, Fu = 65 ksi)
- **Coating:** G90 galvanized
- **Thickness:** 14-16 gauge (0.075" - 0.060")
- **Section:** C-section or Z-section with lips

### Wall Studs (Examples III-7A, III-7B, III-7C)
- **Grade:** ASTM A653 Grade 50 or A1003 SS Grade 50
- **Coating:** G60 galvanized
- **Thickness:** 18-20 gauge (0.048" - 0.036")
- **Section:** C-section or track

### Standing Seam Roof (Example II-3)
- **Grade:** ASTM A792 Grade 50 (Fy = 50 ksi)
- **Coating:** AZ50 or AZ55
- **Thickness:** 22-24 gauge (0.030" - 0.024")
- **Section:** Hat section or rib profile

### Deck (Example I-20)
- **Grade:** ASTM A653 Grade 33 or 40
- **Coating:** G60 or G90
- **Thickness:** 18-22 gauge
- **Section:** Cellular deck profile

## Selection Guide

**Choose steel grade based on:**

1. **Strength requirements:**
   - Light loads → Grade 33
   - Typical structural → Grade 50
   - High loads → Grade 80

2. **Environment:**
   - Indoor, dry → Bare or light coating (G40)
   - Normal outdoor → G60 or G90
   - Coastal/harsh → A792 (Al-Zn)

3. **Application:**
   - Framing → A1003 or A653
   - Roofing → A653 or A792
   - Deck → A653

4. **Ductility needs:**
   - Connections, seismic → Type H (higher ductility)
   - Standard → Type H or Class 1
   - Non-critical → Type L or Class 3
