# J.3 BOLTS

## J.3.1 Holes and Slots for Bolts

The nominal diameter of holes for bolts shall not be more than 1/16 in. (2 mm) greater than the nominal diameter of the bolt unless slip-critical connections are used.

The nominal width of slots for bolts shall not be more than 1/16 in. (2 mm) greater than the nominal diameter of the bolt. If the nominal length of the slot exceeds 2.5$D$ or the edge distance is less than 1.5$D$, where $D$ is the nominal bolt diameter, the edge distance perpendicular to the slot length and slot length shall be sized to avoid overstressing the material along the slot. Unless slip-critical connections are used, the length shall be perpendicular to the direction of force.

## J.3.2 Minimum Spacing of Bolts

The distance between bolt centers shall not be less than 2.5 times the nominal diameter of the bolt.

## J.3.3 Minimum Edge Distance of Bolts

The distance from the center of a bolt to an edge of a part shall not be less than 1.5 times the nominal diameter of the bolt. See Section J.3.6 for the effect of edge distance on bearing strength.

## J.3.4 Bolt Tension

The design tension strength ($\phi R_n$ for LRFD and $R_n/\Omega$ for ASD) of aluminum bolts shall be determined for the limit state of tensile rupture as follows:

$$\phi = 0.65 \text{ (LRFD)}$$
$$\Omega = 2.34 \text{ (ASD)}$$

# RIGHT COLUMN

$$R_n = (\pi D - 1.191/n)^2/4)F_{tu} \tag{J.3-1}$$

where

$F_{tu}$ = tensile ultimate strength of the bolt (Table A.4.7 or Table A.4.7M).

## J.3.5 Bolt Shear

The available shear strength ($\phi R_n$ for LRFD and $R_n/\Omega$ for ASD) of aluminum bolts shall be determined for the limit state of shear rupture as follows:

$$\phi = 0.65 \text{ (LRFD)}$$
$$\Omega = 2.34 \text{ (ASD)}$$

a) For bolts with threads in the shear plane,

$$R_n = (\pi D - 1.191/n)^2/4)F_{su} \tag{J.3-2}$$

b) For bolts without threads in the shear plane,

$$R_n = (\pi D^2/4)F_{su} \tag{J.3-3}$$

where

$F_{su}$ = shear ultimate strength of the bolt (Table A.4.7 or Table A.4.7M).

If the grip (total thickness of parts being fastened) of an aluminum bolt exceeds 4.5$D$, the bolt's nominal shear strength shall be reduced by dividing by $1/2 + G_f/(9D)$ where $G_f$ is the grip and $D$ is the bolt's nominal diameter.

## J.3.6 Bolt Bearing

The available bearing strength ($\phi R_n$ for LRFD and $R_n/\Omega$ for ASD) of connected parts shall be determined for the limit state of bearing as follows:

$$\phi = 0.75 \text{ (LRFD)}$$
$$\Omega = 1.95 \text{ (ASD)}$$

a) For a bolt in a hole,

$$R_n = d_e t F_{su} \le 2DtF_{su} \tag{J.3-4}$$

b) For a bolt in a slot with the slot perpendicular to the direction of force:

$$R_n = 1.33DtF_{su} \tag{J.3-5}$$

and the edge distance perpendicular to the slot length and slot length shall be sized to avoid overstressing the material between the slot and the edge of the part.

where

$d_e$ = distance from the center of the bolt to the edge of the part in the direction of force.

$t$ = for plain holes, thickness of the connected part; for countersunk holes, thickness of the connected part less ½ the countersink depth

## J.3.7 Slip-Critical Bolted Connections

### J.3.7.1 General

Slip-critical connections shall be designed as connections between aluminum and steel members shall comply with the design requirements for Structural Connections (RCSC SC). Connections between aluminum and other metals shall comply with ASTM A193 Grade B8, B8C, B8M; except as modified herein. Slip-critical connections shall be designed in accordance with the slip factors and safety factors in Section J.6, and slip in accordance with Section J.3.7.3.

### J.3.7.2 Special Considerations

Aluminum used in slip-critical connections shall have a minimum Brinell hardness number of 60 (HB). There are no requirements on slip resistance when the surfaces are stainless steel or ASTM A193 Grade 3B, and washers shall comply with manufacturer's recommended torque or in accordance with manufacturer's specification.

Friction-type connections shall be designed for the limit state of slip in accordance with the Specification for Structural Steel Buildings with the following modifications:

a) For standard size and short slotted holes perpendicular to the direction of the load

$\mu$ = slip coefficient = 0.50

b) For standard size and short slotted holes parallel to the direction of the load

$\mu$ = 0.50 (LRFD)
$\Omega$ = 2.0 (ASD)

$R_n = F_{sy}A_s$  (J.3-6)

where

$F_{sy}$ = shear yield strength

Specifications for Structural Steel Buildings (ANSI/AISC 360). See Section J.3.7.3 for slip factors.

### J.3.7.3 Slip Resistance

Friction-type connections shall be designed for the limit state of slip in accordance with the Specification for Structural Steel Buildings with the following modifications:

a) For standard size and short slotted holes perpendicular to the direction of the load

# RIGHT COLUMN

$\mu$ = 1.50 (ASD)

b) For oversized and short slotted holes parallel to the base metal

$\mu$ = 0.50 (LRFD)
$\Omega$ = 1.75 (ASD)

c) For long slotted holes,

$\mu$ = 0.45 (LRFD)
$\Omega$ = 2.0 (ASD)

d) $\mu$ = mean slip coefficient = 0.50 for aluminum surfaces abraded and cleaned to meet the criteria of "Class A" for aluminum surfaces clean and somewhat oxidized; 0.30 for as-ex-truded aluminum surfaces. In accordance with RCSC SC.

## J.3.8 Lockbolts

Lockbolts shall meet the requirements in this Specification and shall be manufactured, installed, and conform to the lockbolt manufacturer's specifications. The bearing area under the washer shall not exceed that for the bolt and washer assemblies in Section J.6.5.

# J.4 RIVETS

## J.4.1 Holes for Cold-Driven Rivets

The nominal diameter of holes for cold-driven rivets shall be no more than 3.5 percent greater than the nominal diameter of the rivet.

## J.4.2 Minimum Spacing of Rivets

The distance between rivet centers shall be not less than 3 times the nominal diameter of the rivet.

## J.4.3 Minimum Edge Distance of Rivets

The distance from the center of a rivet to an edge of a part shall not be less than 1.5 times the nominal diameter of the rivet. See Section J.4.6 for the effect of edge distance on the bearing strength.

## J.4.4 Rivet Tension

Rivets shall not be used to resist loads acting parallel to the axis of the rivet.

## J.4.5 Rivet Shear

The available shear strength ($\phi R_n$ for LRFD and $R_n/\Omega$ for ASD) of aluminum rivets shall be determined for the limit state of shear rupture as follows:

$$\phi = 0.65 \text{ (LRFD)}$$
$$\Omega = 2.34 \text{ (ASD)}$$

$$R_n = \pi D_h^2 F_{su}/4 \tag{J.4-1}$$

where

$D_h$ = nominal diameter of the hole (See Section J.4.1 for hole size limits and Section J.4.8 for hollow-end rivets).

$F_{su}$ = shear ultimate strength of the rivet (See Table A.4.8).

## J.4.6 Rivet Bearing

The available bearing strength ($\phi R_n$ for LRFD and $R_n/\Omega$ for ASD) shall be determined for the limit state of bearing as follows:

$$\phi = 0.75 \text{ (LRFD)}$$
$$\Omega = 1.95 \text{ (ASD)}$$

$$R_n = d_e t F_{su} \le 2 D_h t F_{su} \tag{J.4-2}$$

where

$d_e$ = distance from the center of the rivet to the edge of the part in the direction of force.

$t$ = for plain holes, nominal thickness of the connected part; for countersunk holes, nominal thickness of the connected part less ½ the countersink depth

$F_{su}$ = tensile ultimate strength of the connected part

$D_h$ = nominal diameter of the hole

## J.4.7 Blind Rivets

Grip lengths and hole sizes for blind rivets shall comply with the manufacturer's specifications.

## J.4.8 Hollow-End (Semi-Tubular) Rivets

The shear strength of hollow-end rivets with solid cross sections for a portion of the length shall be taken equal to the strength of solid rivets of the same material if the bottom of the cavity is at least 25% of the rivet diameter from the plane of shear.

## J.5.2 Minimum Spacing of Screws

The distance between screw centers shall not be less than 2.5 times the nominal diameter of the screw.

## J.5.3 Minimum Edge Distance of Screws

The distance from the center of a screw to an edge of a part shall not be less than 1.5 times the nominal diameter of the screw. See Section J.5.5.1 for the effect of edge distance on the bearing strength.

## J.5.4 Screwed Connection Tension

The tensile strength of a screwed connection is the least of the pull-out, pull-over, and screw tensile rupture strengths. The available tensile strength ($\phi R_n$ for LRFD and $R_n/\Omega$ for ASD) shall be determined as follows:

$$\phi = 0.50 \text{ (LRFD)}$$
$$\Omega = 3.0 \text{ (ASD)}$$

The nominal strength $R_n$ for the limit state of pull-out shall be determined in accordance with Section J.5.4.1.

The nominal strength $R_n$ for the limit state of pull-over shall be determined in accordance with Section J.5.4.2.

The nominal strength $R_n$ for the limit state of screw tensile rupture shall be determined in accordance with Section J.5.4.3.

For screws subjected to tension, the head of the screw or washer, if a washer is provided, shall have a nominal diameter not less than 5/16 in. (8 mm). Washers shall have a nominal thickness not less than 0.050 in. (1.3 mm).

## J.5.4.1 Pull-Out

### J.5.4.1.1 Screws in Holes

The nominal strength $R_n$ for the limit state of pull-out of a screw in a hole is:

a) For UNC and UNF threads (screw types C, D, F, G, and T)

1) for 0.060 in. $\le L_e < 0.125$ in. (1.5 mm $\le L_e < 3$ mm)

$$R_n = K_v D L_e F_{ty2} \tag{J.5-1}$$

where

$K_v = 1.01$ for 0.060 in. $\le L_e < 0.080$ in. (1.5 mm $\le L_e < 2$ mm)
$K_v = 1.20$ for 0.080 in. $\le L_e < 0.125$ in. (2 mm $\le L_e < 3$ mm)

$F_{ty2}$ = tensile yield strength of member not in contact with the screw head or washer

$D$ = nominal diameter of the screw

2) for 0.125 in. $< L_e < 0.25$ in. (3 mm $< L_e < 6.3$ mm)

$$R_n = 1.2D F_{ty2}(0.25 - L_e) + 1.16 A_s F_{tu2}(L_e - 0.125) \tag{J.5-2}$$

where

$A_s$ = thread stripping area of internal thread per unit length of engagement

$F_{tu2}$ = tensile ultimate strength of member not in contact with the screw head or washer

3) for 0.25 in. $< L_e < 0.375$ in. (6.3 mm $\le L_e \le 10$ mm)

$$R_n = 0.58 A_s L_e F_{ty2} \tag{J.5-3}$$

b) For spaced threads (screw types AB, B, BP, BF, and BT)

1) for 0.038 in. $\le L_e \le 2/n$ (1 mm $\le L_e \le 2/n$)

$$R_n = K_v D L_e F_{ty2} \tag{J.5-4}$$

where

$K_v = 1.01$ for 0.038 in. $\le L_e < 0.080$ in. (1 mm $\le L_e < 2$ mm)
$K_v = 1.20$ for 0.080 in. $\le L_e < 2/n$ (2 mm $< L_e < 2/n$)

2) for $2/n < L_e < 4/n$

$$R_n = 1.2D F_{ty2} (4/n - L_e) + 3.26D F_{ty2} (L_e - 2/n) \tag{J.5-5}$$

3) for $4/n \le L_e \le 0.375$ in. ($4/n \le L_e \le 8$ mm)

$$R_n = 1.63D L_e F_{ty2} \tag{J.5-6}$$

### J.5.4.1.3 Screws (1/4 in. Diameter) in Screw Chases

The nominal strength $R_n$ for the limit state of pull-out of a ¼ in. nominal diameter screw in a screw chase is:

$$R_n = K_{sc} L_e F_{su} (n_r/n)^2 w_r \tag{J.5-8}$$

where

$K_{sc}$ = 0.021 in²

$F_{su}$ = tensile ultimate strength of the screw chase extrusion

$n_r$ = 14 threads/in.

$n$ = number of threads/in. of the screw

$L_e$ = length of engagement of the screw's threads in the chase, calculated by deducting any chamfer at the top of the chase and the portion of the fastener at

# RIGHT COLUMN

its point without full threads. The deduction for the point shall be: for AB screws, 0.250 in, for UNC screws, 0.050 in. and for F screws, 0.225 in.

$w_r$ = nominal width of the chase, but no less than 0.180 in. and no greater than 0.220 in. For ribbed-wall chases, the nominal width is the inside width of the chase plus the height of one rib. The ribs' nominal height shall not exceed the depth of the screw thread used in the chase.

The nominal thickness of the chase walls shall not be less than 0.150 in. and the nominal depth of the chase shall not exceed 0.750 in.

### J.5.4.2 Pull-Over

a) The nominal strength $R_n$ for the limit state of pull-over for non-countersunk screws is:

$$R_n = C_{pov} t_1 F_{tu1} (D_{ws} - D_h) \tag{J.5-9}$$

where

$t_1$ = nominal thickness of the part in contact with the screw head or washer

$F_{tu1}$ = tensile ultimate strength of the part in contact with the screw head or washer

$D_h$ = nominal diameter of the hole in the material in contact with the screw head or washer

where

$F_{ty1}$ = tensile yield strength of the part in contact with the screw head or washer

$D_{ws}$ = nominal diameter of the washer. The washer may be integral with the screw head.

## J.5.5 Screwed Connection Shear

The shear strength of a screwed connection is the least of the bearing, tilting, and screw shear rupture strengths. The available shear strength ($\phi R_n$ for LRFD and $R_n/\Omega$ for ASD) shall be determined as follows:

$$\phi = 0.50 \text{ (LRFD)}$$
$$\Omega = 3.0 \text{ (ASD)}$$

The nominal strength $R_n$ for the limit state of bearing shall be determined in accordance with Section J.5.5.1.

The nominal strength $R_n$ for the limit state of tilting shall be determined in accordance with Section J.5.5.2.

The nominal strength $R_n$ for the limit state of screw shear rupture shall be determined in accordance with Section J.5.5.3.

## J.5.5.1 Screw Bearing

The nominal strength $R_n$ for the limit state of bearing is

$$R_n = d_e t F_{su} \le 2DtF_{su} \tag{J.5-13}$$

where

$d_e$ = distance from the center of the screw to the edge of the part in the direction of force.

# RIGHT COLUMN

$t$ = for plain holes, nominal thickness of the connected part; for countersunk holes, nominal thickness of the connected part less ½ the countersink depth.

$F_{su}$ = tensile ultimate strength of the connected part

$D$ = nominal diameter of the screw

## J.5.5.2 Screw Tilting

For $t_2 \le t_1$, the nominal strength $R_n$ for the limit state of tilting is:

$$R_n = 4.2(t_2^2 D)^{1/2} F_{ty2} \tag{J.5-14}$$

where

$t_1$ = nominal thickness of the part in contact with the screw head or washer

$t_2$ = nominal thickness of the part not in contact with the screw head or washer

For $t_2 > t_1$, tilting is not a limit state.

## J.5.5.3 Screw Shear

The nominal strength $R_n$ of an aluminum screw for the limit state of screw shear rupture is:

$$R_n = A_r F_{su} /1.25 \tag{J.5-15}$$

where

$A_r$ = root area of the screw

$F_{su}$ = shear ultimate strength of the screw
= 41 ksi (285 MPa) for 7075-T73 screws
= 37 ksi (255 MPa) for 2024-T4 screws
