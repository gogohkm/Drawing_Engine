#### A.4.1.1 Properties at Elevated Temperatures

Except as noted in Section A.4.1.2, the properties given in Section A.4 apply to material held at temperatures of 200°F (93°C) or less for any period of time and for the alloys and tempers listed in Table A.4.2 held at the temperatures listed in Table A.4.2 for cumulative periods of time no greater than the times listed in Table A.4.2. When alloys not addressed in Table A.4.2 are heated above 200°F or alloy-tempers addressed in Table A.4.2 are heated beyond the time-temperature limits of Table A.4.2, properties used in design shall be adjusted to the properties after heating. Table 4.1 gives the reductions to properties for 6xxx alloys at elevated temperatures and Table 4.2 gives strengths of 6061-T6, 6063-T5, and 6063-T6 at elevated temperatures.

### A.4 MATERIAL (continued)

Interpolate time $(t)$ for other temperatures $T$ using

$$\log t = \log t_x + \frac{\log(T_x / T)}{\log(T_1 / T_i)} \log(t_i / t_x) \quad (A.4-1)$$

where

$T_1$ = next lower temperature in Table A.4.2 than $T$

$T_2$ = next higher temperature in Table A.4.2 than $T$

$t_1$ = time corresponding to $T_1$

$t_2$ = time corresponding to $T_2$

#### A.4.1.2 Temperature Limits

Base metal alloys 5350, 5083, 5086, 5154, and 5456 and weldments made with filler metal alloys 5183, 5356, 5556, or 5654 shall not be subjected to temperatures greater than 150°F (66°C), except during fabrication in accordance with Section M.3.

#### A.4.2 Wrought Products

This *Specification* applies to the wrought products listed in Table A.4.3 and produced to one of the ASTM specifications listed in Table A.4.3M.

The tensile ultimate strength $F_{tu}$, tensile yield strength $F_{ty}$, and tension coefficient $k_t$ of non-weld affected wrought material and the tensile ultimate strength $F_{tuw}$ and tensile yield strength $F_{tyw}$ of weld-affected wrought material used to determine available strength shall be as listed in Table A.4.3 or A.4.3M. The tensile ultimate strength $F_{tuwz}$ and tensile yield strength $F_{tywz}$ of the weld-affected zone used to determine available strength is the weighted average of the strengths of the weld-affected wrought material and the filler metal as determined by equations A.4-2 and A.4-3, respectively.

$$F_{tuwz} = (F_{tuw}BM A_{BM} + F_{tuw}FM A_{FM})/A_{wz} \quad (A.4-2)$$

$$F_{tywz} = (F_{tyw}BM A_{BM} + F_{tyw}FM A_{FM})/A_{wz} \quad (A.4-3)$$

where

$F_{tuw}BM$ = tensile ultimate strength of the weld-affected base metal given in Table A.4.3 or A.4.3M

$F_{tuw}FM$ = tensile ultimate strength of the filler metal given in Table A.4.6.

$F_{tyw}BM$ = tensile yield strength of the weld-affected base metal given in Table A.4.3 or A.4.3M

$F_{tyw}FM$ = tensile yield strength of the filler metal given in Table A.4.6.

$A_{BM}$ = area of the base metal in the weld-affected zone

$A_{FM}$ = area of the filler metal

$A_{wz}$ = area of the weld-affected zone, which extends 1 in. (25 mm) from the centerline of a groove weld or the heel of a fillet weld

$$A_{wz} = A_{BM} + A_{FM} \quad (A.4-4)$$
