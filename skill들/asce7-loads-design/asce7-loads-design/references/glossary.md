# ASCE 7-22 Glossary

## A

**Allowable Stress Design (ASD)**
Design method using service-level loads with reduced allowable stresses. Also called Working Stress Design. Defined in Section 2.4.

**Approximate Period (Ta)**
Estimated fundamental period of structure using simplified formulas (Eq. 12.8-7). Used in seismic design when detailed analysis not performed.

## B

**Base Shear (V)**
Total lateral seismic force at the base of the structure. Calculated using Eq. 12.8-1: V = CsW

**Basic Wind Speed (V)**
3-second gust wind speed at 33 ft (10 m) above ground in open terrain (Exposure C). Mapped values in ASCE 7.

## C

**Components and Cladding (C&C)**
Elements of building envelope not part of main wind force resisting system. Designed separately for localized wind pressures.

**Cs (Seismic Response Coefficient)**
Coefficient relating base shear to effective seismic weight. Calculated per Section 12.8.1.1.

## D

**Dead Load (D)**
Weight of all permanent construction, including structural and nonstructural components. Section 3.1.

**Design Spectral Response Acceleration (SDS, SD1)**
- SDS: Short-period (0.2 sec) design spectral acceleration
- SD1: Long-period (1.0 sec) design spectral acceleration
Used to determine Seismic Design Category and seismic forces.

**Directional Procedure**
Wind load analysis method accounting for wind direction effects. Method 2 (Chapter 26-27) or Method 4.

**Drift (Δ)**
Lateral displacement between floors. Story drift is displacement of one level relative to level below.

## E

**Effective Seismic Weight (W)**
Total dead load plus applicable portions of other loads used in seismic analysis. Per Section 12.7.2.

**Equivalent Lateral Force (ELF)**
Simplified seismic analysis method distributing lateral forces based on structural characteristics. Section 12.8.

**Exposure Category (B, C, D)**
Classification of ground surface roughness affecting wind loads:
- B: Urban/suburban with numerous obstructions
- C: Open terrain with scattered obstructions (most common)
- D: Flat, unobstructed areas (water, desert)

## F

**Flood Load (Fa)**
Loads from flooding, including hydrostatic, hydrodynamic, wave, and debris impact. Chapter 5.

**Fundamental Period (T)**
Period of vibration of structure in direction under consideration. Can be calculated (Ta) or from analysis.

## G

**Ground Snow Load (pg)**
50-year ground snow load determined from maps or site-specific data. Section 7.2.

**Gust Effect Factor (G, Gf)**
Factor accounting for dynamic wind effects and turbulence. Section 26.11.

## I

**Importance Factor (Ie, Is)**
- Ie: Seismic importance factor based on Risk Category
- Is: Snow importance factor based on Risk Category

**Irregularity**
Structural discontinuity or nonuniformity in geometry, mass, or stiffness:
- Horizontal irregularity: Plan irregularity (Table 12.3-1)
- Vertical irregularity: Elevation irregularity (Table 12.3-2)

## L

**Live Load (L)**
Load produced by use and occupancy, not including construction loads, environmental loads, or dead loads. Section 4.3.

**Load and Resistance Factor Design (LRFD)**
Design method using factored loads and factored resistances. Also called Strength Design. Section 2.3.

## M

**Main Wind Force Resisting System (MWFRS)**
Structural elements providing support and stability for overall structure. Distinct from components and cladding.

**MCER (Risk-Targeted Maximum Considered Earthquake)**
Ground motion with 1% probability of collapse in 50 years. Basis for design ground motion.

## O

**Overstrength Factor (Ω0)**
Factor to account for structural overstrength beyond design strength. Used for capacity design of certain elements.

## P

**P-Delta Effects**
Secondary effects from deformation of structure under vertical loads. Must be considered when story drift ratio exceeds limits.

## R

**Rain Load (R)**
Load from rainwater accumulation on low-slope roofs. Section 8.3.

**Redundancy Factor (ρ)**
Factor accounting for structural redundancy in seismic design. Section 12.3.4.

**Response Modification Coefficient (R)**
Factor reflecting ductility, overstrength, and damping of structural system. Table 12.2-1.

**Risk Category (I, II, III, IV)**
Building classification based on occupancy and function:
- I: Low hazard to human life
- II: Standard occupancy (most buildings)
- III: Substantial hazard to human life
- IV: Essential facilities

## S

**Seismic Design Category (SDC) (A through F)**
Classification determining required seismic design provisions. Based on Risk Category and spectral response. Section 11.6.

**Site Class (A through F)**
Soil classification based on shear wave velocity, SPT, or undrained shear strength. Chapter 20.

**Snow Drift**
Accumulation of snow from wind transport. Leeward, windward, and roof step drifts. Sections 7.6-7.9.

**Story Drift Ratio**
Drift of one story divided by story height. Limited by Table 12.12-1.

## T

**Torsion**
Twisting of structure about vertical axis. Accidental torsion per Section 12.8.4.2.

**Tsunami**
Series of waves generated by underwater disturbance. Design per Chapter 6 for Tsunami Risk Categories II, III, IV.

## V

**Velocity Pressure (qz)**
Wind pressure at height z calculated from wind speed. Eq. 26.10-1:
qz = 0.00256 Kz Kzt Kd Ke V²

## W

**Wind-on-Ice Load (Wi)**
Combined effect of ice accumulation and wind on iced surface. Section 10.4.4.
