Every seismically isolated structure and every portion thereof shall be designed and constructed in accordance with the requirements of this section and the applicable requirements of this standard.

**SEISMICALLY ISOLATED STRUCTURE:** A structure in which an isolation system has been placed between the superstructure and the ground or between discrete components of a structure to cause a reduction in ground-induced accelerations reaching parts of the structure.

**WIND-RESTRAINT SYSTEM:** The collection of structural elements that provides restraint of the seismic-isolated structure under wind loads.

The wind-restraint system permits movements of the isolated structure under earthquake loads and may be the same as the isolation system, but does not include the isolation system.

**17.2.2 Configuration** Each isolated structure shall be designed for the structure above the isolation system has Type 1 irregular configuration above the isolation system in the direction under consideration.

**EXCEPTION:** The structure above the isolation system is permitted to be taller than the height limit of Category Type 1, where F and Table 12.2-1 in either orthogonal direction, provided all of the following conditions are met:

1. A dynamic analysis above the isolation system shall be provided to demonstrate that the seismic force-resisting system provides adequate stiffness and strength to limit interstory drifts and forces in the structure.

2. The isolated structure shall have a monitored condition system in accordance with Section 17.2.4.

**17.2.3 Fire Resistance** Fire-resistance ratings for isolated structures shall exceed the requirements for similar non-isolated structures by one hour. Where required for isolated structures over three stories in height, fire resistance shall be based on requirements for the structure above the isolation system in Section 17.2.8.1.

**17.2.3.1 Fire Residence** The structure above the isolation system shall meet the fire protection requirements of the applicable building codes in the structure above the isolation system. Fire resistance for isolated structures shall be 2 hours.

**17.2.4 Displacement Restraints** The seismically isolated structure shall be provided with lateral displacement restraints to limit lateral displacement when subjected to service loads. These displacement restraints shall have adequate strength to withstand the loads required as calculated using the requirements for wind design, and shall not restrain the structure from displacing in response to the MCE<sub>R</sub> response.

**17.2.4.1 Vertical-Load Stability** Each element of the isolation system and displacement restraints shall have a monitored vertical load carrying capacity in excess of 1.5 times the maximum vertical load transmitted by the element.

**17.2.4.2 Vertical Load Capacity** To preclude failures from nonseismic loads, each element of the isolation system shall meet requirements of Chapter 11 and limiting design criteria in accordance with Section 17.8.

**17.2.4.3 Inspection and Replacement** The following items shall apply to inspection and replacement of the isolation system:

1. Access for inspection and replacement of all components of the isolation system shall be provided.

2. A registered design professional responsible for design of the isolated structure shall specify and provide sufficient inspection, maintenance, testing, documentation, quality assurance, and quality control for installation of isolation system components.

3. All monitoring, testing, replacement, lubrication, and servicing requirements for the isolation system.

4. Replacement schedule for wear of and replacement of the isolation system.

**17.2.4.4 Quality Control** A quality control testing program for manufactured components of the isolation system shall be established by the registered design professional responsible for the structural design, incorporating quality control testing by an independent engineering specialist.

## 17.4 ANALYSIS PROCEDURE SELECTION

The isolation system and structure shall be modeled and analyzed using the procedures of either Section 17.5 or Section 17.6. Where supplementary viscous dampers are used as part of the isolation system, the procedure of Section 17.6 shall be used.

**17.4.1 Equivalent Lateral Force Procedure.** The equivalent lateral force procedure shall be permitted for the design of the isolation system, and the isolated structure where all of the following conditions are satisfied. Where the equivalent lateral force procedure is used, an explicit upper-bound force-deflection behavior shall be evaluated separately for upper bound and lower bound isolation system properties.

1. The structure is located on a Site Class A, B, C, or D site.
2. The structure is not more than four stories or 65 ft (20 m) in height above the base level and the period of the structure above the isolation system is less than or equal to $T_s$ (T$_s$ = 0.8 s) in the direction under consideration.
3. The effective period of the isolated structure at the maximum displacement, $T_M$, is less than or equal to 3.0 s.
4. The effective damping of the isolation system at the maximum displacement, $\beta_M$, is less than or equal to 30% of critical.
5. The structure above the isolation system does not have significant torsional irregularities, as defined in Section 12.3.2.1 Type 1a and 1b. Significant torsion means the distance between the center of rigidity and the center of mass is more than 5% of the maximum dimension perpendicular to the direction of loading.
6. The effective stiffness of the isolation system at the maximum displacement is greater than 50% of the effective stiffness at 20% of the maximum displacement.

The isolation system is capable of producing a recentering force as defined in Section 17.2.8.3, and the force is specified in Section 17.2.8.4.

where $D_M$ is the maximum displacement, $D_D$ is the design displacement, and $T_D$ is the effective period of the isolation system at the design displacement, all as defined in Section 17.2.2.

**17.4.1.1 Extreme Torsional Irregularity Exception.** For structures with an extreme torsional irregularity, as defined in Section 12.3.2.1, Type 1a or 1b, the equivalent lateral force procedure of Section 17.5 shall be permitted to be used, provided all of the following conditions are satisfied:

1. The effective damping of 30% of the maximum displacement, $D_M$, shall be determined using upper bound and lower bound isolation system properties in accordance with Equation (17.5-3).
2. The total maximum displacement, $D_{TM}$, shall be taken as

$$D_{TM} = D_M \left(1 + y\frac{12e}{b^2 + d^2}\right) \tag{17.5-2}$$

(a) The isolation system does not limit maximum earthquake displacements to less than the total maximum displacement, $D_{TM}$.
