**30.1.1 Building Types.** This chapter applies to the determination of wind loads on components and cladding (C&C) on buildings.

1. Part 1: Low-rise buildings, defined in Section 26.2, having eave height not exceeding 60 ft (18.3 m), roof slope not exceeding 45 degrees;
2. Low-rise building type definitions in Section 28.2;
3. Part 2: Buildings with a mean roof height exceeding 60 ft (18.3 m), and the side pressures are calculated from a wind pressure approach, building walls enclosed buildings for walls from Section 26.2;
4. Part 3: Buildings that do not meet the requirements above, pressures are calculated for a wind pressure;
5. Rooftop solar panels with wind speeds derived from this chapter;
6. Part 4: Building appurtenances such as roof overhangs, parapets, exterior windows, on piers, chimneys, tanks, circular bins, silos, open equipment, roof-mounted solar panels and roof pressure;
7. Part 5: Parapets;
8. Part 6: Roof overhangs; and
9. Ground-mounted solar panels which roof slopes less than or equal to 7 degrees, and
10. Ground-mounted solar panels which roof is designed using Chapter 26.

## 30.1.2 Conditions

A building that has design wind loads specified in conditions listed below is within the scope of this chapter. The chapter applies to

1. The building is a regular-shaped building as defined in Section 26.2; and
2. The building does not have response characteristics making it subject to across-wind, torsional, vortex shedding, or buffeting; and is not have a site condition that falls under one of the categories of Section 26.5.1(b), does not have a site subject to channeling effects, does not have extreme shielding or buffeting considerations.

**30.1.3 Limitations.** The provisions of this chapter take into consideration the load magnification effect caused by gusts in resonance with along-wind vibrations of flexible buildings and structures that do not exceed the requirements of Section 26.2 for height, height-to-width ratios, and natural frequency, or where a more detailed analysis is made. For structures not meeting these requirements, refer to recognized literature for determination of wind loads and other features.

**30.1.4 Shielding.** There shall be no reductions in velocity pressures caused by apparent shielding afforded by buildings

resistance with along-wind vibrations of flexible buildings, building heights, roof lines, cross-section areas and wind and
considerations shall be determined using recognized literature

**30.2 GENERAL REQUIREMENTS**

The Wind Load Parameters specified in Chapter 26, The general requirements shall be determined in accordance with the following shall be determined in accordance with Chapter 26.

- Basic wind speed, V (Section 26.5);
- Wind directionality factor, Kd (Section 26.6);
- Exposure category B, C, or D (Section 26.6);
- Topographic factor, Kzt (Section 26.8);
- Ground elevation factor, Ke (Section 26.9);
- Velocity pressure velocity pressure coefficient (Section 26.10); Kz or Kh
- Gust-effect factor, G or Gf (Section 26.11); and
- Enclosure classification (Section 26.12).

**30.2.2 Minimum Design Wind Pressures** The design wind pressures for C&C shall be positive (inward) and shall meet the requirements specified in Section 26.11. The design method shall be permitted to be designed using the provisions for main wind load systems.

**30.2.3 Wind Pressure Acting on Opposite Faces of Each Building Surface** For buildings with pressure coefficients, $(GC_p)$, and $(GC_{pi})$ shall be applied to the associated factor and external wind pressure coefficients $(GC_p)$ are given in the figures associated with this chapter, combined net pressures on each building surface shall be determined as:

$$p = q_h[(GC_p) - (GC_{pi})] \text{ (lb/ft}^2\text{)} \tag{30.2-1}$$

$$p = q_h[(GC_p) - (GC_{pi})] \text{ (N/m}^2\text{)} \tag{30.2-1.SI}$$

where $(GC_p)$ is for rooftop structures and equipment with $h$ less than 3.0 ft $(h < 0.9 \text{ m})$ shall be permitted to be reduced from $1.8$ for $L_c/h = 0$ to the value of $h$ is increased beyond the height of the structure, as shown with ASCE 7.

### 30.3 BUILDING TYPES

The provisions of this section are applicable to an enclosed, partially enclosed, or partially open low-rise building.

- Low-rise building type, definition in Section 26.2; or
- Roofed multispan gable, sawtooth, or monoslope roof (per roof, monoslope roof, or sawtooth roof); or
- Roofed with multispan hip roof with $\theta \leq 27°$ (see Section 30.3.4).

### 30.3.1 Design Wind Pressures

Design wind pressures on C&C shall be determined by the following equation:

$$p = q_h [(GC_p) - (GC_{pi})](lb/ft^2)$$ (30.3-1)

$$p = q_h [(GC_p) - (GC_{pi})](N/m^2)$$ (30.3-1-SI)

where

- $q_h$ = Velocity pressure evaluated at mean roof height h as defined in Section 26.10.2; and
- $GC_p$ = Wind pressure coefficient at mean roof height h as defined in Section 26.2 and
  - Figures 30.3-1 to 30.3-7 (roofs)
  - Figures 30.3-2A-C (Flat roofs, gable roofs, and hip roofs)
  - Figures 30.3-3 (stepped roofs)
  - Figures 30.3-4A-D (monoslope roofs),
  - Figures 30.3-5A-B (monoslope surface of elevated buildings)
  - Figure 30.3-6 (sawtooth roofs)
  - Figure 30.3-7A (gable roofs)
  - Figure 30.3-8A (hip roofs)
  - Figure 30.3-9A (stepped roofs)
  - Figure 30.3-10 (arched roofs), and
- $GC_{pi}$ = internal pressure coefficient

#### 30.3.2.1 Relative Horizontal Surface of Elevated Buildings

Exposed horizontal surface of elevated buildings shall be designed in accordance with Figure 30.3-1, with the following modifications:

1. In ASCE/SEI 7-10, method 2, the horizontal value of $C_p$ shall be taken as the larger of
   - $C_p = +0.8$ or $C_p = -0.8$
2. In method 1, the horizontal surface is treated as walls with $GC_p$ shall equal the larger of the determining zone dimensions.
