**21.1.1 Base Ground Motion** Consistent with Section 11.4.7, the determination of ground motion at any depth below the surface shall be in accordance with Section 21.1.2. The analysis shall be developed using the procedure of Section 11.4.8, Section 11.4.9, or Section 11.4.10, using the stiffness and damping properties of the soil profile specified at the site location or from ground motion prediction equations at rock, using site response analysis per Section 21.1.2 and 21.2, or using ground-based methods for the risk-targeted maximum considered earthquake motion, and then the adjustments per Section 11.4.8 shall be made for determining response in accordance with Equations (11.4-13) and (11.4-14). For sites where bedrock is very deep and constitutes below 5000 ft (1500 m), the response spectrum analysis shall be performed using the procedures of Section 11.4.9. Where site-specific studies are used, an adjustment per Equations (11.4-13) and (11.4-14) is not required if the deterministic floor shall be chosen to be 5% probabilistic fragility in Equations (11.4-13) and (11.4-14), or the seismic design categories shall be determined using the procedures of Section 11.4.1 through 11.4.3.
