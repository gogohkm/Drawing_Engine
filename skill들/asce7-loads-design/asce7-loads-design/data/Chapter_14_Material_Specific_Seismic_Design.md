## 14.1 STEEL

Building structures, including foundations, composed of steel to resist seismic forces shall be designed and detailed in accordance with the requirements of Chapters 12 and 13, the structures and additional requirements provided in this section.

**14.1.1 Reference Documents** The quality, testing, design, and construction of steel members and their connections in the seismic-force-resisting system shall be governed by the applicable reference documents cited in this section: ANSI/AISC 360, ANSI/AISC 341, and ANSI/AISC 358, ANSI/AISC 360, ASCE 8, and ASCE 19.

**14.1.2 Seismic Requirements for Structural Steel Structures** The design of structural steel members shall be in accordance with ANSI/AISC 360. Where structures fall within certain categories as defined in Seismic Design Category B, C, D, E, or F of Section 11.6, the design shall comply with additional seismic requirements of Section 11.7.

**14.1.2.1 Seismic Requirements for Structural Steel Structures** in Seismic Design Category B: The structures with their structural systems in Seismic Design Category B, through Seismic Design Category B, C, or F, shall be designed and detailed in accordance with Section 13.6, except as permitted in Table 15.4-1.

**14.1.2.2 Seismic Requirements for Structural Steel Structures** Structural steel structures composed of structural steel with their system assigned to Seismic Design Category C, D, E, or F shall be designed and detailed in accordance with ANSI/AISC 341.

**EXCEPTION:** The response modification coefficient, $R$, for the system of Section 11.7 applicable to steel systems composed of listed special seismic systems, the following steel systems shall be designed and detailed in accordance with ANSI 8289 and used not be designed and detailed in accordance with ANSI/AISC 341:

1. Seismic force-resisting systems for structures with $R$ = 3 associated with Section 12.2.

## 14.3 COMPOSITE STEEL AND CONCRETE STRUCTURES

Seismic requirements for structures having composite steel and concrete to resist seismic forces shall be designed and detailed in accordance with ANSI/AISC 341.

**14.3.1 Reference Documents** The design, construction, and quality control of composite steel and concrete structures, such as composite column members, composite beam members, encased composite column members, filled composite column members, composite diaphragms, and composite joints, shall conform to the requirements of ANSI/AISC 360, ANSI/AISC 341, and ACI 318, with the following additions and exceptions.

## 14.4 MASONRY

Masonry structures, including foundations, composed of masonry to resist seismic forces shall be designed and detailed in accordance with the provisions of Chapter 12, the references, and the applicable provisions of TMS 402.

**14.4.1 Reference Documents** The design, construction, and quality control of masonry structures and their components shall conform to the applicable provisions of the reference documents listed in Chapter 23.

**14.4.2 IF Factors** To qualify for the response modification coefficients, $R$, the height to be derived shall comply with TMS 402 and shall comply with the additional provisions of Sections 1.1.6.2.4 and 1.3.5.3 of TMS 402.

Special reinforced masonry shear walls designed in accordance with Section 1.1.6.2.4 of TMS 402 shall comply with the requirements of Section 1.14.4.5.3 and 1.3.5.3 of TMS 402.

## 14.5 WOOD

Structures, including foundations, constructed of wood to resist seismic loads shall be designed and detailed in accordance with this standard including the references and additional requirements provided in this section.

**14.5.1 Reference Documents** The quality, testing, design, and construction of wood members and their fastenings in systems that resist seismic forces shall conform to the requirements of the applicable following reference documents: AWC NDS and AWC SDPWS.
