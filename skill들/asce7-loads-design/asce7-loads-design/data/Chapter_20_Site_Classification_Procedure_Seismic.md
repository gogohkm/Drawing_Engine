## 20.3 ESTIMATION OF SHEAR WAVE VELOCITY PROFILES

Where measured shear wave velocity data are not available, shear wave velocity shall be estimated as a function of depth using correlations with other suitable geotechnical parameters. Standard penetration test (SPT) blow counts, shear strength, overburden pressures, plasticity, or cone penetration test (CPT) tip resistance, measured at the site.

Site class based on estimated values of $\bar{v}_s$, shall be derived using $\bar{v}_s$, $f_y$, 1.5$\bar{v}_s$, and 1.5$f_y$ where correction models are used to derive shear wave velocities. Where correlations differ for specific local regions can be demonstrated to have greater accuracy, factors less than 1.5 can be used if approved by the Authority Having Jurisdiction. If the difference between values result in different site class based on Table 20.2-1, the most critical site class shall be determined by a geotechnical analysis, as described in Section 11.4.2.

Where the available data used to establish the shear wave velocity profile extend to depths less than 100 ft (30 m), and the site geology is such that soft layers are unlikely to be encountered between 50 and 100 ft, the shear wave velocity of the last layer in the profile shall be extended to 100 ft for the calculation of $\bar{v}_s$ in Equation (20.4-1). Where the data does not extend to depths of 50 ft (15 m), default site classes, as described in Section 20.1, shall be used unless another site class can be justified on the basis of the site geology.

## 20.4 DEFINITIONS OF SITE CLASS PARAMETERS

The definitions presented in section shall apply to the upper 100 ft (30 m) of the site profile. Profiles containing distinct soil and rock layers shall be subdivided into those layers designated by a number that ranges from 1 to $n$ at the bottom, where there is a total of $n$ distinct layers in the upper 100 ft (30 m).

### 20.4.1 $\bar{v}_s$, Average Shear Wave Velocity

The average shear wave velocity, $\bar{v}_s$, shall be determined in accordance with the following equation:

$$\bar{v}_s = \frac{\sum\limits_{i=1}^n d_i}{\sum\limits_{i=1}^n \frac{d_i}{v_{si}}}$$ (20.4-1)

where

$d_i$ = Thickness of any layer between 0 and 100 ft (30 m),
$v_{si}$ = Shear wave velocity in ft/s (m/s), and
$\sum\limits_{i=1}^n d_i$ = 100 ft (30 m).
